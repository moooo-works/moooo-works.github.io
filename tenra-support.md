# Tenra Support / 支援

## English

Thanks for using **Tenra — Film Camera & Darkroom**.

If you have any questions, feedback, or bug reports, please reach out — we usually reply within a few days.

- **Email:** [moooo.works@gmail.com](mailto:moooo.works@gmail.com)

When reporting an issue, it helps to include:

- Your device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 13, iOS 17.5)
- The app version (shown at the bottom of Settings)
- What happened, and the steps to reproduce it

### Common Questions

**My photos disappeared after uninstalling the app.**
Photos are stored locally on your device inside the App. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes them. Please export the photos you want to keep before uninstalling.

**How do I restore my Tenra Pro purchase?**
Open the Tenra Pro page in the App and tap **Restore Purchases**, using the same App Store / Google Play account you purchased with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by your app store, not inside the App:
- **iOS:** Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions
- **Android:** Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Canceling takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

### 常見問題

**解除安裝後照片不見了。**
照片儲存於裝置本機的 App 內。解除安裝 App 會永久刪除照片，請在解除安裝前先匯出想保留的照片。

**如何恢復 Tenra Pro 購買？**
在 App 內開啟 Tenra Pro 頁面，點選「**恢復購買**」，並使用當初購買時的同一個 App Store / Google Play 帳戶。

**如何取消訂閱？**
訂閱由商店管理，不在 App 內操作：
- **iOS：**設定 → Apple 帳號 → 訂閱項目
- **Android：**Play 商店 → 付款與訂閱 → 訂閱

取消將於當期計費週期結束時生效。
