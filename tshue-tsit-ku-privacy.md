# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

**Last updated / 最後更新：2026-08-08**

---

## English

### Overview

"Tshuē Tsi̍t Kù" (the App) is developed by moooo_works. It is a fully offline
search and learning tool for Taiwanese Hokkien proverbs. This policy explains
how we handle information when you use the App.

### Data Stored on Your Device

All data you create in the App — favorites, browsing history, search history,
and appearance/font-size settings — is stored **locally on your device only**
and is never transmitted to our servers. This data is removed when you
uninstall the App. The "export search history" feature is triggered manually
by you, and its content is only copied to your device's clipboard.

### Optional Improvement Program (Off by Default)

The first time you open the App, we ask whether you are willing to share
**anonymous** usage information to help us improve search quality.
**No data is uploaded unless you consent.** If you agree, we collect the
following via Google Firebase:

- Search query text (up to 100 characters) and the number of results
- Which proverb you selected and its rank position in the results
- Crash reports (Crashlytics)

This data does not include your name, phone number, email address, or any
information that directly identifies you. You can disable sharing at any
time from **Settings → Improvement Program**.

Once you consent, Firebase also automatically collects standard usage
statistics (such as app opens and screen views) and device information
(device model, OS version, language, and a coarse region derived from your
IP address), along with an anonymous install identifier. We have disabled
collection of the Advertising ID.

- **Google Firebase** — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not require account registration and do not collect any personally
  identifiable information.
- We do not use advertising SDKs.

### Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | Used only to upload anonymous data for the Improvement Program after you consent; core features work fully offline |

### Data Source Attribution

The original text, definitions, example sentences, and audio of the proverbs
are sourced from the Ministry of Education's *Dictionary of Frequently-Used
Taiwan Minnan* (臺灣台語常用詞辭典), used under the CC BY-ND 3.0 TW license
with no modification to the original text.

### Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the
age of 13.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted
on this page with an updated date.

### Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
**moooo.works@gmail.com**

## 繁體中文

### 概述

「揣一句」(本應用程式)由 moooo_works 開發,是一款純離線的台語俗諺
搜尋與學習工具。本政策說明我們如何處理您使用本應用程式時的資訊。

### 儲存在您裝置上的資料

您在應用程式內產生的所有資料 —— 收藏、瀏覽歷史、查詢記錄、外觀與字級設定 ——
僅儲存在您的裝置本機,不會傳送至我們的伺服器。解除安裝應用程式即一併移除。
「匯出查詢記錄」功能由您手動觸發,內容僅複製到您裝置的剪貼簿。

### 選擇性的改善計畫(預設關閉)

首次開啟應用程式時,我們會詢問您是否願意分享**匿名**使用資訊以協助改善搜尋品質。
**未經您同意,不會有任何資料上傳。** 若您同意,我們透過 Google Firebase 收集:

- 搜尋查詢文字(最長 100 字)與結果數量
- 您點選了哪一句俗諺與其排序位置
- 當機記錄(Crashlytics)

這些資料不含姓名、電話、電子郵件或任何可直接識別您身分的資訊。
您可隨時在「設定 → 改善計畫」關閉分享。

您同意後,Firebase 也會自動收集標準使用統計(如 app 開啟次數、畫面瀏覽次數)
與裝置資訊(機型、作業系統版本、語言,以及由 IP 位址推導的粗略地區),
以及一組匿名的安裝識別碼。我們已停用廣告識別碼(Advertising ID)的收集。

- **Google Firebase** — [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 我們不會收集的資訊

- 不需要註冊帳號,不收集任何個人身分資訊。
- 不使用廣告 SDK。

### 使用的權限

| 權限 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | 僅用於您同意後的匿名改善計畫資料上傳;核心功能完全離線 |
