# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

**Last updated / 最後更新：2026-06-12**

---

## English

### Overview

Tenra ("the App") is a virtual film photography app developed by moooo_works. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

### Your Photos Stay on Your Device

All photos you shoot or import in the App — originals, previews, and developed results — are stored **locally on your device only**. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you import from your photo library, the App copies the selected photos into its private storage; your original photo library is never modified.

### Analytics

The App uses **Google Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymized usage statistics that help us improve the App, such as which features are used (e.g. a roll was loaded, a develop was started) and general device information.

- Analytics events **never** include your photos, file paths, or any text you type (such as roll titles).
- No account registration is required; analytics are tied to an anonymous identifier, not to you personally.
- See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google processes this data.

### Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by the app store you purchased from — the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. We use **RevenueCat** to manage purchase status; it receives purchase tokens/receipts and an anonymous app user ID — never your name, email, or payment details (those stay with Apple or Google).

- **RevenueCat** — [Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Google Play Billing** — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Apple App Store (In-App Purchase)** — [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect, store, or transmit your photos.
- We do not require account registration.
- We do not use advertising SDKs.
- We do not sell any data.

### Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Camera | Shooting frames with the in-app camera |
| Photo library access | Importing photos into a roll, and saving exported images to your album |
| Notifications | A **local** notification when your develop is ready (no data leaves the device) |
| `INTERNET` | Analytics and purchase processing only |

### Local Storage

Photos in Tenra are stored in the App's private internal storage. This data is removed when you uninstall the App or delete a roll. Deleting a roll permanently removes its photos from the App.

### Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

### Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
**moooo.works@gmail.com**

## 繁體中文

### 概述

Tenra（「本應用程式」）是由 moooo_works 開發的虛擬底片攝影應用程式。本政策說明您使用本應用程式時，我們如何處理相關資訊。

### 您的照片只留在您的裝置上

您在本應用程式中拍攝或匯入的所有照片——包括原始檔、預覽與沖洗結果——**僅儲存於您的裝置本機**，絕不會上傳至我們的伺服器或任何第三方。從相簿匯入時，本應用程式會將您選擇的照片複製至應用程式私有儲存空間；您相簿中的原始照片絕不會被修改。

### 數據分析

本應用程式使用 **Google Firebase Analytics** 收集匿名使用統計以協助改善產品，例如功能使用情況（裝片、送洗等事件）與一般裝置資訊。

- 分析事件**絕不**包含您的照片、檔案路徑或任何您輸入的文字（如底片捲命名）。
- 無需帳號註冊；分析資料僅關聯匿名識別碼，不關聯您本人。
- Google 如何處理這些資料，請參閱 [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。

### 購買

訂閱與買斷購買由您購買時使用的應用程式商店處理——**Apple App Store** 或 **Google Play**。我們使用 **RevenueCat** 管理購買狀態；其僅接收購買憑證／收據與匿名應用程式用戶 ID——絕不包含您的姓名、電子郵件或付款資料（這些資訊由 Apple 或 Google 保管）。

- **RevenueCat** — [隱私權政策](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Google Play 帳款服務** — [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Apple App Store（App 內購買）** — [Apple 隱私權政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

### 我們不收集的資料

- 我們不收集、儲存或傳輸您的照片。
- 本應用程式不需要帳號註冊。
- 我們不使用廣告 SDK。
- 我們不出售任何資料。

### 使用的權限

| 權限 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| 相機 | 以應用程式內相機拍攝 |
| 相簿存取 | 將照片匯入底片捲,以及將匯出的影像存入您的相簿 |
| 通知 | 沖洗完成時的**本地**通知（不會有任何資料離開裝置） |
| `INTERNET` | 僅用於數據分析與購買處理 |

### 本機儲存

Tenra 中的照片儲存於應用程式的私有內部儲存空間。解除安裝應用程式或刪除底片捲時，相關資料將一併移除。刪除底片捲會永久移除該捲在應用程式內的照片。

### 兒童隱私

本應用程式不會刻意收集 13 歲以下兒童的任何資訊。

### 政策變更

我們可能不定期更新本隱私權政策。變更內容將連同更新日期發佈於本頁面。

### 聯絡方式

如您對本隱私權政策有任何疑問，請透過以下方式與我們聯絡：
