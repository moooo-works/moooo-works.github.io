# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

**Last updated / 最後更新：2026-04-15**

---

## English

### Overview

KidStars ("the App") is developed by moooo_works. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

### Information We Collect

**Locally stored data (on-device only)**

All data you create in the App — including children's names, avatars, tasks, rewards, and star records — is stored locally on your device using a local database (Room/SQLite). This data is never transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly enable Family Sync (see below).

**Family Sync (Pro feature — optional)**

If you choose to enable Family Sync, the App uses Google Sign-In to authenticate your Google account, and syncs your children's data (names, tasks, rewards, star records) to Firebase Firestore to allow multiple parent devices to share the same data.

- Only data you have created in the App is synced.
- Sync is disabled by default and requires explicit opt-in.
- You can leave the family group at any time from Settings to stop syncing.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect any personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily enter (children's names/nicknames).
- We do not require account registration for basic features.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to third parties.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

### Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Send weekly star report notifications (Android 13+) |
| `INTERNET` | Required for Family Sync and Google Play Billing |
| `VIBRATE` | Haptic feedback on task completion |

### Google Sign-In (Family Sync Only)

If you use Family Sync, the App uses Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) via the Android Credential Manager API. We access only your Google account UID and email address to identify your account within the family group. We do not store your Google credentials.

You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.

- **Google Sign-In** — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Firebase** — [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Google Play Billing

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Pro subscription and one-time purchase) processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. All billing is handled by Google.

- **Google Play** — [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)

### Data Retention

- Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or manually delete it.
- Firestore sync data is retained until you leave the family group or request deletion by contacting us.

### Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be used by parents to manage their children's tasks and rewards. Parents enter their children's names and data. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

### Contact

If you have questions about this policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us at:
**jsl1122@gmail.com**

## 繁體中文

### 概述

KidStars（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）由 moooo_works 開發。本政策說明您在使用本應用程式時，我們如何處理相關資訊。

### 我們收集的資訊

**本機儲存資料（僅存於裝置）**

您在應用程式中建立的所有資料，包括孩子的名字、頭貼、任務、獎勵及集星紀錄，均以本機資料庫（Room/SQLite）儲存於您的裝置上。除非您明確啟用家庭同步功能（見下方），否則這些資料不會傳輸至我們的伺服器。

**家庭同步（Pro 功能 — 選用）**

若您選擇啟用家庭同步，應用程式將使用 Google 登入驗證您的 Google 帳號，並將孩子的資料（名字、任務、獎勵、集星紀錄）同步至 Firebase Firestore，讓多個家長裝置共享同一份資料。

- 僅同步您在應用程式中主動建立的資料。
- 同步功能預設為關閉，需明確開啟。
- 您可隨時在設定頁面離開家庭群組以停止同步。

### 我們不收集的資訊

- 除您自願輸入的資料（孩子名字或暱稱）外，我們不收集任何個人識別資訊。
- 基本功能不需要帳號註冊。
- 我們不使用廣告 SDK，也不將資料出售給第三方。
- 我們不使用分析或崩潰回報 SDK。

### 使用的權限

| 權限 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | 發送每週星星週報通知（Android 13+）|
| `INTERNET` | 家庭同步及 Google Play 結帳所需 |
| `VIBRATE` | 任務完成時的震動回饋 |

### Google 登入（僅限家庭同步）

若您使用家庭同步，應用程式將透過 Android Credential Manager API 使用 Google 登入（OAuth 2.0）。我們僅存取您的 Google 帳號 UID 與電子郵件地址，用於識別家庭群組中的帳號。我們不儲存您的 Google 憑證。

您可隨時在 Google 帳號設定中撤銷授權。

- **Google 登入** — [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Firebase** — [Firebase 隱私權與安全](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Google Play 結帳

本應用程式提供可選的應用程式內購（Pro 訂閱及永久買斷），由 Google Play 處理結帳。我們不接收或儲存您的付款資訊，所有結帳均由 Google 處理。

- **Google Play** — [Google Play 服務條款](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)

### 資料保留

- 本機資料保留於您的裝置，直到您解除安裝應用程式或手動刪除。
- Firestore 同步資料將保留至您離開家庭群組，或透過聯絡我們提出刪除請求。

### 兒童隱私

本應用程式設計供家長管理孩子的任務與獎勵，由家長輸入孩子的名字與相關資料。我們不直接向兒童收集資訊。

### 聯絡方式

如您對本政策有任何疑問或希望申請刪除資料，請聯絡我們：
**jsl1122@gmail.com**
